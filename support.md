# Movie Hangman Support

## Contact

Email **trioadhdlabs@gmail.com** and you'll get a reply. Spotted a wrong title,
year or actor? Send it over too.

## How to play

Pick a language, then guess the film one letter at a time. Right letters fill
every matching blank; spaces, numbers and punctuation show from the start. Six
wrong letters (head, body, two arms, two legs) and the round is over.

## Common questions

**What are the credits under the hangman?**
Clues. The release year is free. "Roll credits" reveals the genre (−10 points),
then the lead star (−15), then the director (−20). After that, "Reveal a
letter" fills in a letter but costs a life.

**How is a round scored?**
100 points, minus 10 per wrong letter and the cost of any credits you rolled,
never less than 10 for a win. Classic films (1950–1989) score ×1.5 and vintage
films (before 1950) ×2. Normal difficulty adds ×1.5 and Hard ×2. Each win in a
row adds a streak bonus of 10 points, up to 50. A loss resets the streak.

**Will I get the same film twice?**
Not until you've played every film in that era at your difficulty. Then that
era starts over, and the game tells you.

**Why are most films recent?**
On purpose: 96% of films come from 1990 onwards, 3.5% from 1950–1989 and 0.5%
from before 1950. Settings → Eras lets you switch any era off.

**What does difficulty change?**
Easy plays only the best-known quarter of each era, Normal the best-known 60%,
Hard every film.

**Why are Hindi, Tamil, Japanese… titles in English letters?**
Every language plays on the same A–Z keyboard, so titles use their common
romanized spelling, as on posters and streaming services.

**Can I type on a keyboard?**
Yes. On iPad with a keyboard and on Mac, type a letter to guess it and press
Return for the next film.

**How do I start over?**
Settings → Reset scores and streaks.
